Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 May 2026

This privacy policy explains how Share Business Concepts ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you interact with our website, our Instagram and Facebook pages, and related services. By using our services or interacting with our content, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who we are

Share Business Concepts is an Indian education business that offers structured trading and investing programs. Our primary website is sharebusinessconcepts.com. You can reach us at tech@sharebusinessconcepts.com.

2. What information we collect

2.1 Application form

When you submit our program application form, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include: full name, email, date of birth, gender, WhatsApp number, city, country, occupation, trading experience, markets traded, and other answers to the application questions.

2.2 Instagram and Facebook interactions

When you publicly comment on our Instagram or Facebook posts, our systems may receive the following information from Meta's webhook events:

  • The Instagram or Facebook user ID and username of the commenter
  • The text of the public comment
  • The post (media) ID the comment was made on
  • Timestamps and metadata associated with the comment

If your public comment contains specific keywords related to our programs (for example, asking about a class), our systems may automatically reply to your comment publicly and send you a one-time direct message containing program information. We do not read or store private direct messages outside of these automated interactions.

2.3 Website analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand aggregate traffic and user behaviour. These tools may set cookies and collect IP-derived approximate location, browser, device, and interaction data. We do not use this analytics data to identify individuals.

3. How we use the information

  • To respond to applications and contact applicants about our programs.
  • To automatically respond to comment-based queries on our public Instagram and Facebook content with relevant program information.
  • To measure the effectiveness of our content and outreach (aggregate analytics on number of comments received, reply success rate, etc.).
  • To improve our website and services.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. How we store and protect information

Application form responses are stored securely in our backend systems and accessed only by authorised members of our team. Comment metadata (user ID, username, comment text, timestamps) is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database in encrypted form, accessible only to our authorised systems via authenticated API calls. We retain this information only as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your information. We share information only with:

  • Meta Platforms (Facebook, Instagram) — when our systems make API calls on our behalf to reply to comments or send direct messages, the necessary fields are passed back to Meta to complete those operations. This is governed by Meta's own platform terms and privacy policies.
  • Infrastructure providers (Cloudflare, Google) — these providers process data on our behalf to host our website, run our backend, and provide analytics. They are bound by their own data-protection terms.
  • Authorities — if required by applicable law, court order, or to protect our rights.

6. Your rights

You have the right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. To exercise any of these rights, email tech@sharebusinessconcepts.com with the subject line "Data Request" and include the email address, Instagram handle, or Facebook profile you used when interacting with us. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data deletion

To request deletion of any personal data we hold about you, including comment metadata captured from your interactions with our Instagram or Facebook pages, email tech@sharebusinessconcepts.com with the subject line "Delete My Data". Include the platform (Instagram or Facebook) and the username or user ID you wish to be deleted. We will confirm deletion within 30 days.

8. Children

Our services are not intended for use by children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated through our website.

10. Contact

For any questions about this policy or our data practices, email tech@sharebusinessconcepts.com.
